Wrap ২০২৬ — Screen Guide

Every screen, its elements, and how it looks for three kinds of student — Best, Good and Low — side by side. Read top to bottom.

▶ Open the live prototype (v=b)

What it is

  • A tap-through খাতা that plays a student their own 2026 — study, classes, practice, and their learner-type.
  • 12 screens exist; each student sees a curated 7–8, in a server-driven order.
  • It ends on a shareable marksheet.

The three students (on every screen)

Best · আরিফ

Very active — the fullest version.

Good · নিশাত

A solid, typical student.

Low · রাফি

Little activity — still encouraged, never empty.

● Primary = always on the screen  ·  ○ Secondary = only when the data exists.

✓

Rules for every screen

A few things true everywhere, so no single screen has to reinvent them.

  1. 1
    Low, even zero → encourage

    Keep the screen, show a warm line. Never a bare “0”, never an empty chart, never a fake badge.

  2. 2
    Data genuinely absent → sit out one slide

    Only that slide (rare). We never guess or fake data.

  3. 3
    Under 5 active days → Fresh Start

    The kind 2-slide deck instead of a hollow full one.

  4. 4
    No photo → generic avatar

    A clean male/female avatar — never a blank or a wrong face.

  5. 5
    Shared image is identity-safe

    No name, photo, school or rank by default — many students are minors.

  6. 6
    Missing/long name, missing value

    Generic headline (never “null”), trim ~18 chars, show “--” (never “0”).

  7. 7
    Bangla-first, motion-safe

    Renders in Bangla with Bengali numerals even in an English app; works fully with motion off.

  8. 8
    Everyone finishes proud

    Best, Good or quiet — lead with effort, never shame a number.
