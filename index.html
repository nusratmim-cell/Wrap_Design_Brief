Year-End Wrap ২০২৬ — Design Brief

A short, personal tap-through recap of a student’s 2026 on Shikho — their hours, classes, practice and the kind of learner they were — ending on a card they can share. This brief says what each screen must do and what’s yours to design.

▶ Open the live prototype (v=b)

The v=b prototype is a reference, not a mandate — just one way this flow can look. Treat its layout, motion and art as a starting point you’re free to depart from. What’s binding is each screen’s Must-show items and the Rules true on every screen; the visual direction is yours.

What it is

  • A full-screen খাতা (notebook) that plays a student their own year, one idea per screen.
  • 12 screens exist; each student sees a curated 7–8, in a server-driven order — the thin ones are left out.
  • It opens from the existing home banner and ends on a shareable marksheet.
  • Android only · Bangla-first (Bengali numerals) · runs 26–31 December 2026.

The flow is an emotional ramp: personal → reflective → rewarding → shareable, peaking at the learner-type reveal (Screen 09). The goal reaction is “আরে, আমি এত কিছু করেছি!” — recognition, not a stream of compliments.

Navigation & actions. It’s a tap-through story (like a Stories deck): a progress bar at the top, tap right / left or swipe to move between slides — so the story slides (01–08) carry no on-slide buttons. Real buttons appear only where a choice is offered: the banner (00), the reveal (09), the marksheet (10) and the close (11). On each of those, the UI team’s rule holds — exactly one primary + one secondary, and every such screen below spells out its Primary and Secondary explicitly.

Three activity levels (shown on each screen)

Illustrative sample profiles, used to show how a screen adapts. Neutral by design — no student is “better”.

High activity

Most slides, real badges, a full marksheet.

Moderate

Meaningful slides; thin ones omitted; no forced badges.

Limited

Honest numbers, no fake praise; under 5 active days → Fresh Start.

✓

Rules true on every screen

So no single screen has to reinvent them.

  1. 1
    Honest, not falsely positive

    Low-but-real → show the number plainly, no manufactured praise. A number can stand on its own.

  2. 2
    Thin single metric → drop that one slide

    Below its threshold (min_show) the slide sits out — we never fake or spin it. The student still gets a full flow.

  3. 3
    Under 5 active days → Fresh Start

    The kind 2-slide deck, not a set of hollow stat screens.

  4. 4
    No photo → generic avatar

    A clean male/female avatar — never a blank or a wrong face.

  5. 5
    Shared image is identity-safe

    No name, photo, school or rank by default — many students are minors.

  6. 6
    Missing/long name, missing value

    Generic headline (never “null”), trim long names cleanly, omit empty rows (never “0” / “--”).

  7. 7
    Bangla-first, motion-safe

    Renders in Bangla with Bengali numerals even in an English app; fully works with motion off.

  8. 8
    Accurate to the metric

    Most-active day ≠ “best day”; most-time subject ≠ “strongest”; active days ≠ a streak. Say exactly what the data means.
